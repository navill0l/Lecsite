House Rules

The League of Legends club has a few rules to make sure everyone enjoys their stay:

Clean as you go
Our place is operated by the community, please take part in maintaining cleanliness in our place!
Don't get too heated
Don't get us wrong, banter is very fun! But when things start getting heated a lot of the times people get hurt, so calm down when you're feeling the heat!
Have fun!
The reason why we're all here is to have fun playing League of Legends, the goal of our club is to make sure all of us are having fun!
